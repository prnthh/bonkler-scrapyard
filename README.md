# Features 
The Bonkler Scrapyard has many features! Even a few which were not included in the original creator!

### Configuring
Choose every component of your Bonkler, from the background to the head, body, and weapons. Customize these aspects to your liking. Realize your vision.

### Building
Watch your Bonkler creations live. Every choice you make is reflected almost instantly, allowing you to see your creation develop step by step.

### Randomizing
Like adventure? Click the Random button to generate a completely unique Bonkler. Each random build is a surprise and features a (probably) never-before-seen combination of properties.

### Saving
Once you're satisfied with your sculpted Bonkler, you can easily save it to your hard drive or mobile device to preserve your creation for posterity or future inspiration.

### Sharing
Share on social media platforms, email to family members directly, or share the URL with friends. Let your creativity shine and inspire others with a personalized Bonkler.

### Naming
Use the field at the top to name your Bonkler, this field was not previously used in the original Bonkler creator, but now it affects the file name if you save the Bonkler.

### Setting a reserve price
Another unique feature of Scrapyard, for the number-inclined autistics or believers in angel numbers, is that we allow you to set a floor price that will be displayed full screen (numbers over 99.9 may look like an old-fashioned Numbers in video games are just as broken).
